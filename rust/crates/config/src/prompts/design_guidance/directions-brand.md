# Brand directions

Eight directions for brand-led and expressive products. Each entry fills every field; a proposal repeats the fields for each direction it presents. Directions are starting points: each design must still pass the rules unless the brief or design system sets another value, and then the deviation is listed. Blend at most two.

## Poster
F1: mood (three words, one-line idea): loud, confident, memorable; one huge statement per screen
F2: surface polarity: either, in strong blocks; state which polarity each section uses
F3: accent discipline: 2 to 3 brand colours only if the brief allows, each with a named job; the action accent stays unique and never fills a block
F4: neutrals: 2 to 3 tinted steps: a near-black and an off-white
F5: type roles and sizes per surface: display face 72 to 160 px in the hero, tightly set, with a plain text face at 18 to 20 px; caps only for short labels
F6: shape language: sharp corners or fully round, one of the two for the whole design
F7: depth strategy: flat colour blocks; a hard offset shadow is the one optional device
F8: spacing unit and density per surface: 8 px unit, low-medium density, large gaps
F9: imagery role: type as image plus one cropped photograph or illustration
F10: signature moves: an oversized word, overlap, edge-to-edge blocks
F11: forbidden moves (3-6, testable): no centred stack of equal cards; no gradients; no more than 3 colours in a view; no overlap on interactive controls; no body text over an image without a scrim
F12: responsive plan: hero type scales with the viewport inside set limits; blocks re-order instead of stacking; targets 44 px; body at least 16 px
F13: state coverage: hover shifts a block or its offset, a thick high-contrast focus ring, active, disabled, loading, empty, error
F14: fallbacks: name a substitute display face; if none exists use a heavy system sans with tightened tracking
F15: motion: large-scale moves on entry up to 500 ms with one easing; reduced motion shows the static state

## Quiet Luxury
F1: mood (three words, one-line idea): restrained, sparse, slow; one thing per view, given room
F2: surface polarity: deep neutral surfaces, either polarity, never both in one view
F3: accent discipline: a single metallic or earth accent used rarely, for the primary action and a few links
F4: neutrals: deep neutrals in 4 to 5 steps with a warm or cool tint
F5: type roles and sizes per surface: high-contrast serif or fine sans for display, a quiet sans for text; hero 56 to 96 px, text 17 to 18 px; small caps with wide spacing for labels
F6: shape language: sharp or nearly sharp corners, 0 to 2 px
F7: depth strategy: white space and hairlines; no shadows
F8: spacing unit and density per surface: 8 px unit, low density, margins of 64 px or more on wide screens
F9: imagery role: large photography or product imagery with a consistent crop and light
F10: signature moves: a thin rule above headings; long image-led sections; a single quiet call to action
F11: forbidden moves (3-6, testable): no urgency badges; no more than 3 sizes in view; no icons in a grid; no bright accents; no motion that shouts
F12: responsive plan: margins shrink but never vanish; images keep their ratio; one element per row on narrow screens; targets 44 px
F13: state coverage: hover as a slow underline or fade, a clear focus ring in the accent, active, disabled, loading with a calm skeleton, empty, error written politely
F14: fallbacks: name a serif and a sans substitute; note that fine weights may need a heavier fallback to stay legible
F15: motion: fades of 300 to 500 ms with one easing; no parallax; reduced motion uses cuts

## Storefront
F1: mood (three words, one-line idea): clear, appetising, quick; the product sells itself
F2: surface polarity: light, so the product images carry the colour
F3: accent discipline: one accent for the buy action and the cart; never for sale badges or prices
F4: neutrals: white or soft neutral in 5 steps
F5: type roles and sizes per surface: friendly sans for text, a display face for collections and prices; hero 40 to 64 px, product titles 16 to 18 px, prices 18 to 24 px; a smaller price size in dense lists
F6: shape language: radius 6 to 8 px on cards and buttons; images keep their own crop
F7: depth strategy: hairlines and one lift on hover for product cards
F8: spacing unit and density per surface: 8 px unit, medium density; admin or filter areas use a 4 px unit and higher density
F9: imagery role: product photography with consistent aspect ratios and backgrounds
F10: signature moves: image-led grids; a sticky cart and filter bar; reviews summarised in a single line
F11: forbidden moves (3-6, testable): no more than one badge per card; no countdown timers by default; no auto-rotating banners; no placeholder-only form labels; no hidden prices
F12: responsive plan: grids go from 4 to 3 to 2 columns, never 1 for product lists; filters in a sheet; targets 44 px
F13: state coverage: hover with a second image or lift, focus ring, selected variants, disabled out-of-stock, skeleton cards, empty cart with a next step, error on payment with a clear fix
F14: fallbacks: system sans; name a substitute for the display face
F15: motion: hover lift 150 ms; cart add feedback 200 ms; no looping banners; reduced motion drops the lift

## Playground
F1: mood (three words, one-line idea): warm, encouraging, playful; friendly without being childish
F2: surface polarity: light with soft backgrounds; dark optional
F3: accent discipline: up to three colours with named jobs if the brief allows, otherwise one accent plus tints; actions stay one colour
F4: neutrals: soft tinted neutrals in 4 to 5 steps, kept high-contrast for text
F5: type roles and sizes per surface: rounded or humanist sans; hero 40 to 56 px, text 17 to 18 px, short copy
F6: shape language: generous radius 12 to 20 px; pills allowed for buttons and tags
F7: depth strategy: soft tonal layers; no heavy shadows
F8: spacing unit and density per surface: 8 px unit, low-medium density, large touch targets
F9: imagery role: illustrations in one consistent style; real photos of users where possible
F10: signature moves: varied card sizes; friendly empty states; a progress marker with a small celebration on success
F11: forbidden moves (3-6, testable): no finance or safety-critical screens in this style; no more than three bright colours in a view; no mixed illustration styles; no text below 14 px; no confetti on errors
F12: responsive plan: cards reflow to a single column; targets 48 px; illustrations scale but keep their cropping
F13: state coverage: hover bounce limited to 150 ms, thick focus ring, active press, disabled softened but legible, loading with a friendly skeleton, empty with an illustration and a next step, error that blames nothing
F14: fallbacks: name a rounded sans substitute; keep line height generous in the fallback
F15: motion: small springy moves up to 300 ms tied to success or progress; no looping; reduced motion fades

## Zine
F1: mood (three words, one-line idea): handmade, opinionated, expressive; it looks made by a person
F2: surface polarity: paper tones or dark ink, either, by section
F3: accent discipline: one action accent, limited extra colours with named jobs; the accent never colours texture or decoration
F4: neutrals: paper and ink neutrals in 4 steps, with a printed or grainy feel
F5: type roles and sizes per surface: two faces only, one expressive for display and one plain for text; hero 56 to 120 px, text 17 to 18 px; uneven baselines in headings only
F6: shape language: torn, cut or hard-edged shapes for decoration; controls keep 0 to 4 px radius
F7: depth strategy: flat layers, paper-like offsets or tape devices as the one decorative device
F8: spacing unit and density per surface: 8 px unit, medium density; a collage grid with deliberate irregularity
F9: imagery role: photocopied or scanned imagery, hand-drawn marks, cut-out photographs
F10: signature moves: collage layouts; stickers or stamps for categories; rotated headings
F11: forbidden moves (3-6, testable): no rotated or overlapping elements on interactive controls; no rotated body text; no more than 2 faces; no textures behind text; no clutter that hides navigation
F12: responsive plan: the collage simplifies into a single column on narrow screens with a few accents kept; targets 44 px
F13: state coverage: hover as a sticker peel or underline, thick focus ring, active, disabled, loading, empty, error
F14: fallbacks: name a plain substitute for the expressive face and check it keeps the mood
F15: motion: small cut-paper steps of 100 to 200 ms; no continuous motion; reduced motion shows the static layout

## Field Notes
F1: mood (three words, one-line idea): thoughtful, human, reading first; a quiet place to read
F2: surface polarity: warm paper by default; dark as a soft ink-on-charcoal variant
F3: accent discipline: one muted accent for links and the active marker; nothing else
F4: neutrals: warm paper neutrals in 4 to 5 steps and an ink text colour
F5: type roles and sizes per surface: serif or humanist text face at 18 to 20 px on a measure of 60 to 70 characters with a distinct sans for UI labels; headings 28 to 40 px
F6: shape language: radius 0 to 4 px; rules and quotes carry the shape
F7: depth strategy: hairlines and white space; no shadows
F8: spacing unit and density per surface: 8 px unit, low density, generous margins with side notes
F9: imagery role: figures with captions, diagrams and portraits; no decorative photography
F10: signature moves: a single reading column; side notes in the margin; drop caps or pull quotes used sparingly
F11: forbidden moves (3-6, testable): no more than one pull quote per screen; no sidebars that crowd the column; no all-caps passages; no auto-playing media; no ads inside the text
F12: responsive plan: side notes fold into inline notes on narrow screens; the measure holds at 45 to 75 characters; targets 44 px
F13: state coverage: hover underline on links, focus ring, visited links, disabled, loading with text placeholders, empty, error
F14: fallbacks: name a serif and a sans substitute with similar x-height
F15: motion: almost none; a 150 ms fade for the table of contents; reduced motion removes it

## Plain Speech
F1: mood (three words, one-line idea): clear, respectful, low drama; it works for everyone
F2: surface polarity: light, with high-contrast text on white
F3: accent discipline: one strong link and action colour plus a separate visible focus colour
F4: neutrals: 2 to 3 neutral steps with very high contrast
F5: type roles and sizes per surface: one highly legible sans at 18 px or more for text, headings 24 to 36 px in plain words; short lines
F6: shape language: radius 0 to 4 px; clear box outlines on inputs
F7: depth strategy: solid borders and white space; no shadows
F8: spacing unit and density per surface: 8 px unit, low density, one question per page where possible in a narrow column
F9: imagery role: none, or a plain diagram when it explains a step
F10: signature moves: a progress marker and a back link on every step; a check-your-answers summary; plain-language errors
F11: forbidden moves (3-6, testable): no placeholder-only labels; no timeouts without warning; no icons without text; no colour-only status; no jargon in headings
F12: responsive plan: the layout is a single narrow column at every width; targets 44 px or more; no horizontal scrolling at 320 CSS px
F13: state coverage: hover underline, a thick high-contrast focus state, active, disabled with an explanation, loading with text, empty, an error summary at the top that links to each field
F14: fallbacks: system sans; keep size and weight in the fallback
F15: motion: none beyond focus and state changes; reduced motion has nothing to remove

## Throwback
F1: mood (three words, one-line idea): nostalgic, tactile, knowing; it wears its era honestly
F2: surface polarity: era-true: usually dark slabs with light chrome layers; state which
F3: accent discipline: one warm action colour for the primary action; era colours are decoration and never fill actions
F4: neutrals: a small era-true set in 4 to 6 steps with layers (chrome, slab, panel)
F5: type roles and sizes per surface: one era-appropriate family with treatments (case, outline, offset) for character, and an optional pixel face for tiny labels only; body at least 16 px
F6: shape language: hard bevels and square corners; no soft rounding
F7: depth strategy: bevels, inset borders and stepped tones; no soft shadows
F8: spacing unit and density per surface: a fixed-width or table-like canvas on a 4 px unit, medium density
F9: imagery role: period-style illustration, textures and icons in one set
F10: signature moves: bevelled buttons; a title bar or banner motif; a pixel or sprite accent
F11: forbidden moves (3-6, testable): no soft shadows; no gradients that were not in the era; no pixel face for body text; no text below 12 px; no tiny targets
F12: responsive plan: a modern port reflows at 320 CSS px, re-composes the narrow layout in the era's character rather than a plain stack, and enlarges targets to 44 px on touch
F13: state coverage: hover bevel inversion, a strong focus ring, active press, disabled, loading with a period-style bar, empty, error
F14: fallbacks: name substitutes for the period faces; say which textures cannot be reproduced
F15: motion: stepped, frame-like motion of 100 to 200 ms; no smooth easing; reduced motion shows static frames
F16: period variant (Throwback only): keep the palette, bevels and type treatments of the era; modernise target size, reflow, keyboard focus and contrast; list each dropped period quirk
