One PM. A crew of agents. Your merge.

How it's rigged

  1. Youtasks · answers
  2. talks to
  3. PM agentone conversation
  4. cstan
  5. Controllerdaemon · SQLite ledger
  6. tasks
    • Architect
    • Developers · Designers
    • Reviewers · Testers
    • Supervisor
  8. integrate
  9. integration branchsquashed, reviewed
  10. you merge
  11. your HEAD
  1. You ask the PM. It picks a tier; larger work goes to the Architect as packages.
  2. Workers build on their own branches and run cstan report.
  3. Reviews are independent: a fresh reviewer at the reported commit.
  4. Integration is mechanical: no checkout touched, conflicts become tasks.
  5. You merge, then cstan integrate confirm.

First run

cstan init
Creates .capstan/, a starter capstan.toml and roles/designer.md.
cstan config check
Validates the config and prints what it resolved.
cstan start
Starts the controller and launches the PM in Herdr.
cstan dash
Optional read-only dashboard of agents, queue and findings.
