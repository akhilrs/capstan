# Logos

The pen-and-ink capstan with the hand-lettered wordmark, copied unchanged from `docs/assets/` in the repository.

- `capstan-logo-dark.svg`: for dark grounds (`bg` dark). Ink `#e4ded0`, rope `#e0a45e`.
- `capstan-logo-light.svg`: for light grounds. Ink `#22303c`, rope `#a3672a`.
- `capstan-icon.svg`: the square mark on a pale tile, for favicons and avatars.

Show the logo at 32px to 64px tall, with at least `space-4` clear on every side. Do not recolour, outline or redraw it. Swap the dark and light files with the theme.
